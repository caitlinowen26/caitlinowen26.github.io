Engineering Features

Basic step by step of how our case works!

Modules included in our product!

Real time tracking technology

Pressure sensor: Used a small microphone. This sent a message to the Arduino telling that the user was using the vape. 

Solenoid Mechanics: The pressure sensor sent a message to the Arduino, the Arduino sent the solenoid valve information if the user has enough puffs left to open the valve.

Bluetooth Modules: This allows the vape device to communicate with our app, ensuring the user's puffs are being tracked and displayed.  

CAD & 3D Printing

Utilized CAD to design the intricate parts of our vape case. 3D printing where the vape slides into, the case that holds all of the internal pieces, and the mouthpiece lid. 

Coding

Used Xcode to develop an IOS app. Used bluetooth to connect the app and vape device effectively.
