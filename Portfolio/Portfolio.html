Stand Up for Climate Comedy

For the 10th anniversary of Stand Up for Climate Comedy, I co-produced, researched, wrote original comedy sketches, and performed in a live show at the Boulder Theater. Throughout the project, I worked alongside professional comedians to transform climate research into engaging and accessible comedy.

02 CREATIVE CLIMATE COMMUNICATION

Sustainable Fashion Campaign

I designed a sustainable fashion advertisement that was displayed on RTD buses throughout Boulder. The campaign combined original photography, graphic design, and environmental messaging to encourage people to think differently about clothing consumption and the environmental impacts of fast fashion.

The advertisement included a QR code connecting viewers to resources about sustainable fashion, local thrift stores, and Boulder's circular economy initiatives.

Graphic Design / Photography / Advertising / Climate Communication
